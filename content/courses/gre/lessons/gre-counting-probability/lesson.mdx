<Intuition>
Counting problems all start from one principle: if one choice can be made in m ways and a
second in n ways, together they can be made in m × n ways. Permutations and combinations
are just that principle plus one question — **does order matter?** Probability is then
“favorable outcomes over total outcomes,” and for anything that says “at least one,” the
fastest path is almost always 1 minus the probability of none.
</Intuition>

<Concept>
## The multiplication principle

Fill slots one at a time and multiply the number of options for each. A code of 2 letters
followed by 3 digits, repeats allowed: 26 × 26 × 10 × 10 × 10 = 676,000.
</Concept>

<TradeoffTable id="gre-counting" />

<Example title="Order matters or not?">
From 8 club members, how many ways can you choose (a) a president, secretary, and
treasurer, (b) a 3-person committee?

<Solution>
(a) Distinct roles → order matters: 8 × 7 × 6 = **336**.

(b) A committee is just a group → order doesn’t matter. Each committee was counted 3! = 6
times in (a), so 336 ÷ 6 = **56** = C(8, 3).
</Solution>
</Example>

<Concept>
## Restrictions

- **Must be together:** glue them into one block, arrange the blocks, then multiply by the
  ways to arrange inside the block. 5 people in a row with A and B together:
  4! × 2! = 48.
- **Must not be together:** total − together = 5! − 48 = 72.
- **At least one:** total − none. Committees of 3 from 5 men and 4 women with at least one
  woman: C(9, 3) − C(5, 3) = 84 − 10 = 74.
</Concept>

<Check id="gre-cnt-restrict" />

<Concept>
## Probability

P(event) = favorable outcomes ÷ total outcomes (when outcomes are equally likely).

- **AND** (both happen), independent events: multiply. P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B).
- **OR** (at least one happens): P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A and B).
- **NOT**: P(not A) = 1 − P(A).
- **Without replacement:** the second draw’s probabilities change.
</Concept>

<Example title="Without replacement">
A bag has 4 red and 6 blue marbles. Two are drawn without replacement. What is the
probability both are red?

<Solution>
First red: 4/10. Then 3 reds among 9 marbles: 3/9. Multiply: 4/10 × 3/9 = 12/90 = **2/15**.

(With replacement it would be 4/10 × 4/10 = 4/25.)
</Solution>
</Example>

<Concept>
## Overlapping sets

**Total = A + B − Both + Neither.** Subtract “both” once, because people in both groups
were counted twice.
</Concept>

<Gotcha title="Trap — adding probabilities for “and”">
“Both” means multiply; adding gives a number that is too big (and can exceed 1). If your
probability is bigger than 1, something went wrong.
</Gotcha>
